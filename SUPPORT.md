# Support

Shod's Create and TacZ Bridge is a hobby mod with **best-effort support**: no deadlines and no guaranteed fixes.

- **Bugs:** open a [bug report](../../issues/new/choose) with your versions and `logs/latest.log`.
- **Ideas and questions:** leave a comment on the Modrinth or CurseForge page.
- **Compatibility with other mods:** only Create and TacZ are supported. Other gun packs may work through the config (`ammoFamilyOverrides`), but they are not tested.

Reports without a log, or that cannot be reproduced, may be closed.
